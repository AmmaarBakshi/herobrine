## Assets

All textures in this repository are original and generated by the scripts in
[`tools/`](tools/). No Mojang or third-party mod assets are used.

| Asset | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/herobrine/icon.png` | `tools/gen_icon.py` (procedural, fixed seed) | MIT |
| `data/herobrine/structure/berserk/memorial.nbt` | `tools/gen_berserk.py` (hand-laid, fixed seed) | MIT |

| `assets/herobrine/sounds/*.ogg` (arrival, drone, heartbeat, jumpscare, shatter, whisper) | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized, fixed seeds) | MIT |

### Herobrine's voice

Every voice line (`assets/herobrine/sounds/voice/`) is recorded by the mod's developer in their own
voice. No synthetic or AI-generated voices are used. Lines not recorded yet are shown as captions.
The script is in [docs/VOICE_SCRIPT.md](docs/VOICE_SCRIPT.md).
