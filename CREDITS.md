## Assets

Every texture and sound in this repository is original, generated by the scripts in
[`tools/`](tools/) from fixed seeds. No Mojang or third-party mod assets are used, and
nothing is sampled from recordings.

| Asset | Source | License |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/herobrine/icon.png` | `tools/gen_icon.py` (procedural) | MIT |
| `textures/entity/herobrine/herobrine.png` | `tools/gen_textures.py` (original skin, player layout) | MIT |
| `textures/entity/herobrine/herobrine_eyes.png` | `tools/gen_textures.py` (emissive eyes layer) | MIT |
| `textures/item/herobrine_spawn_egg.png` | `tools/gen_textures.py` | MIT |
| `textures/item/mirror_shard.png` | `tools/gen_textures.py` | MIT |
| `sounds/jumpscare.ogg` | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized) | MIT |
| `sounds/drone.ogg` | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized) | MIT |
| `sounds/whisper.ogg` | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized) | MIT |
| `sounds/heartbeat.ogg` | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized) | MIT |
| `sounds/arrival.ogg` | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized) | MIT |
| `sounds/shatter.ogg` | `tools/gen_sounds.py` (synthesized) | MIT |

The sound generator needs `numpy` and `soundfile`; the texture and icon generators need
`Pillow`. The Vorbis encoder is not bit-exact across runs, so regenerate only the sounds
you change: `python tools/gen_sounds.py arrival shatter`.
